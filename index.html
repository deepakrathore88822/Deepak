Key Interview Answers:

1. Why Demucs before Whisper? Normal speech-to-text models fail on songs because heavy bass and drums drown out vocals. Demucs isolates human voice for 98% transcription accuracy.
2. Real-time Scrolling: Achieved through HTML5 video timeupdate event listener mapped to timestamp data attributes and DOM scrollIntoView({ behavior: 'smooth', block: 'center' }).
